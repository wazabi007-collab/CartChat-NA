# 13. Refunds without breaking your books

🎬 **Flow + screen recording** — Flow makes the opener and closer; you record the app in the middle. Flow cannot render the real dashboard.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | VAT-registered and careful merchants |
| **Why this one** | Trust signal. Serious businesses need this. |
| **Length** | 20s |
| **Format** | 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 |

---

## Hook (first 2 seconds — this decides everything)

> **You refunded a customer. Now what does your VAT say?**

Most people watch with the sound off. The hook must be **on screen as text**,
big, in the first frame — not spoken.

## On-screen text timeline

```
  0:00  "You gave a customer their money back."
  0:03  "Now your books don't match your bank."
  0:07  "Record the refund → credit note, automatically."
  0:13  "VAT reversed. Statement updated."
```

## Voiceover script

```
VO:
"You refunded a customer. Good.
 But now your books say one thing and your bank says another.
 Record the refund on the order — OshiCart writes the credit note for you,
 reverses the VAT on it, and updates your statement.
 Your books still balance."

SCREEN RECORDING: Orders → Record refund → amount + date → credit note appears → open it
```
