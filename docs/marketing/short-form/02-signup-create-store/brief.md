# 02. Sign up — your shop online in under 10 minutes

🎬 **Flow + screen recording** — Flow makes the opener and closer; you record the app in the middle. Flow cannot render the real dashboard.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | First-time merchants |
| **Why this one** | Remove the fear that going online is hard or technical. |
| **Length** | 30s |
| **Format** | 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 |

---

## Hook (first 2 seconds — this decides everything)

> **No website. No developer. No card needed.**

Most people watch with the sound off. The hook must be **on screen as text**,
big, in the first frame — not spoken.

## On-screen text timeline

```
  0:00  "Getting your shop online in Namibia:"
  0:02  "❌ website  ❌ developer  ❌ N$5000"
  0:05  "✅ your phone  ✅ 10 minutes"
  0:08  SCREEN RECORDING begins
  0:26  "Free to start. oshicart.com"
```

## Voiceover script

```
VO:
"Everyone says you need a website. You don't.
 Open oshicart.com on your phone. Enter your shop name.
 Choose what you sell. Add your WhatsApp number.
 Pick how customers pay you — EFT, eWallet, cash.
 That's it. Your shop is online, and the link is yours."

SCREEN RECORDING SHOT LIST (record on a real phone, portrait):
  1. oshicart.com homepage → tap Create Free Store
  2. Signup form (blur your email)
  3. Setup step 1: store name + what you sell
  4. Setup step 2: WhatsApp number + town
  5. Setup step 3: payment methods
  6. Land on the dashboard → tap Preview store
Speed the recording to 2-3x, keep taps visible.
```
