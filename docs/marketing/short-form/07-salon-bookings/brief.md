# 07. Salons & barbers — clients book themselves in

🎬 **Flow + screen recording** — Flow makes the opener and closer; you record the app in the middle. Flow cannot render the real dashboard.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Salons, barbers, nail techs, spas |
| **Why this one** | Own the services segment. Booking is the hook, not products. |
| **Length** | 25s |
| **Format** | 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 |

---

## Hook (first 2 seconds — this decides everything)

> **Stop asking "what time works for you?" 40 times a day.**

Most people watch with the sound off. The hook must be **on screen as text**,
big, in the first frame — not spoken.

## On-screen text timeline

```
  0:00  "'What time works for you?'"
  0:02  "×40 a day."
  0:05  "Let them book themselves."
  0:10  SCREEN: calendar → pick day → pick time
  0:20  "Two people can never take the same slot."
```

## Voiceover script

```
VO:
"How many times a day do you type 'what time works for you'?
 Put your hours in once. Your clients pick their own slot from a calendar.
 Taken times disappear — two people can never book the same chair.
 Closing Monday? Block the day. Nobody can book it."

SCREEN RECORDING SHOT LIST:
  1. Storefront → tap a service → Add to cart
  2. Checkout shows the month calendar
  3. Tap a day → time slots appear, one greyed out as 'booked'
  4. Pick a time → place the booking
  5. Dashboard → Bookings calendar showing the new appointment
```
