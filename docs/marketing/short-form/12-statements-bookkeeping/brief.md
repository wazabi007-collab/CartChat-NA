## Hook (first 2 seconds — this decides everything)

> **Your accountant asks for last month. You open a shoebox.**

Most people watch with the sound off. The hook must be **on screen as text**,
big, in the first frame — not spoken.

## On-screen text timeline

```
  0:00  "'Just send me last month.'"
  0:03  (shoebox of receipts)
  0:06  "Or… one tap."
  0:10  "Every order. VAT totals. What came in, what's owed."
  0:17  "Print it or send the spreadsheet."
```
