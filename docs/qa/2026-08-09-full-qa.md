## Manual checklist — needs a merchant login (≈20 min on a phone)

Use a service-industry test account (or Design Today with Neo).

**The bug fix that must be confirmed first**
1. Log in → land on Dashboard, NOT the setup wizard. Open Products, add a
   second item, open Dashboard again. Never bounced to setup. *(The redirect
   bug fixed 9 Aug — this is the regression check.)*

**Availability & bookings**
2. Settings → Availability: tick days, use **Generate** (09:00–17:00, 1 hour),
   save. Times list fills.
3. New **Bookings** page: month grid renders; arrows move months.
4. Tap a day → "Block whole day". Open your own store as a customer (incognito),
   add a service → that day is struck through in the calendar.
5. Unblock; hold back one time instead → only that time greys out at checkout.
6. Book a slot as the customer → it appears in Bookings with your name and
   time; the slot shows "booked" and can't be blocked.

**Services**
7. Add a service, choosing each of the three "where does it happen" options;
   edit it afterwards and change the option.
8. As a customer: an online service shows "Your Booking", no delivery choice,
   no couriers. An at-client service asks "Where should we come to?" and adds
   the call-out fee if you set one in Settings.

**Money screens**
9. Orders: payment status chip shows; "Record payment" saves an amount + date;
   part payment shows "X of Y".
10. Statements (needs Automate/Pro): month renders, Received/Outstanding block
    shows, CSV downloads, print preview looks like a document. On Pro: "Last
    12 months" appears in the picker.
11. An invoice from the order screen: prints on one sheet, correct totals.

**Odds and ends**
12. More menu (mobile bottom bar) lists every section and scrolls.
13. Coupons: apply a template, save, code appears.
14. CSV import: template downloads, a 2-row file imports.
15. Storefront owner bar shows "My dashboard" on your own store; sign out and
    confirm shoppers don't see it.
