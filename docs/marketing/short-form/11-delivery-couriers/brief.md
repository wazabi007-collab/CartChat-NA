# 11. Delivery, collection, or courier — you choose

🎬 **Google Flow only** — no screen recording needed.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Merchants worried about logistics |
| **Why this one** | Show flexibility. Delivery is often the blocker to selling online. |
| **Length** | 20s |
| **Format** | 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 |

---

## Hook (first 2 seconds — this decides everything)

> **"But I can't deliver." You don't have to.**

Most people watch with the sound off. The hook must be **on screen as text**,
big, in the first frame — not spoken.

## On-screen text timeline

```
  0:00  "'I can't deliver.'"
  0:03  "Then don't."
  0:05  "Collection · your own delivery · Yango · inDrive"
  0:12  "The buyer pays the driver. Not you."
```

## Voiceover script

```
VO:
"'I can't deliver' is the reason most shops stay offline.
 So don't. Let customers collect.
 Or set your own delivery fee. Or let them book Yango or inDrive —
 the buyer pays the driver directly, so it costs you nothing."
```
