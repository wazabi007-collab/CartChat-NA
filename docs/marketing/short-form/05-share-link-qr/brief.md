# 05. Your link and QR code — your shop's address

🎬 **Flow + screen recording** — Flow makes the opener and closer; you record the app in the middle. Flow cannot render the real dashboard.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Merchants who don't know how to drive traffic |
| **Why this one** | Teach that the link IS the website, and the QR is for the real world. |
| **Length** | 22s |
| **Format** | 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 |

---

## Hook (first 2 seconds — this decides everything)

> **This link is your website. No domain. No hosting.**

Most people watch with the sound off. The hook must be **on screen as text**,
big, in the first frame — not spoken.

## On-screen text timeline

```
  0:00  "oshicart.com/s/your-shop"
  0:03  "That's your website. That's it."
  0:08  "Put the QR on your door, your packaging, your car."
  0:18  "Share it on Status today."
```

## Voiceover script

```
VO:
"Every OshiCart shop gets its own link. That link is your website —
 no domain to buy, nothing to host.
 Put it in your WhatsApp Status, your bio, your flyers.
 And your QR code goes on the door, the packaging, the back of the bakkie.
 Someone scans it — they're in your shop."
```
