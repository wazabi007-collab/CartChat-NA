# OshiCart short-form video pack — 15 videos for Facebook & TikTok

Each numbered folder holds one complete `brief.md`: hook, on-screen text with
timings, voiceover script, Google Flow prompts, a still-image prompt, and
ready-to-paste captions for both platforms.

---

## Read this before you generate anything

**Google Flow cannot render the OshiCart app.** It generates cinematic footage
from a text description — it has never seen your dashboard, and asking it to
will produce convincing-looking nonsense: fake buttons, garbled text, a product
that isn't yours. That is worse than no video, because it teaches people the
wrong interface.

So the pack splits in two:

| Type | How it's made | Videos |
|---|---|---|
| 🎬 **Flow only** | Fully generated. Story, emotion, brand. | 01, 03, 06, 11, 14, 15 |
| 🎬 **Flow + screen recording** | Flow makes the opener and closer; **you** record the real app in the middle. | 02, 04, 05, 07, 08, 09, 10, 12, 13 |

Each brief marks which it is, and the screen-recording ones include a shot list.

**Recording the app:** use a real phone in portrait, screen-record at
oshicart.com, and speed it to 2–3×. Use the **demo store**
(`oshicart.com/s/oshicart-demo`) so you never expose a real merchant's data.

## Keep every claim true

These are all verified against the live product. Say them freely:

- **Zero commission on sales** — you charge a monthly plan, not a cut
- **Free plan, no card needed**
- **EFT, eWallet, MTC Maris, Pay2Cell, PayToday, cash**
- **Yango and inDrive** in Windhoek, Swakopmund and Walvis Bay — the buyer pays
  the driver
- **Agent bounties: N$75 / N$200 / N$400**, paid by EFT, no cap
- **35-day trial** when someone signs up through an agent link (30 otherwise)

**Do not invent:** merchant counts, revenue figures, "join 5,000 sellers",
star ratings, or testimonials from named people. You have a small number of
real merchants — a fabricated number is both dishonest and easy to disprove
locally. When you want social proof, ask a real merchant for a real quote and
their permission.

## Brand consistency

Paste this at the end of **every** Flow prompt so all 15 videos look related:

```
Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920. Warm natural Namibian daylight, documentary realism,
shallow depth of field, gentle handheld motion. Real Namibian people and places
— Windhoek, Oshakati, Swakopmund. Colour palette leans warm sand, deep green
(#159947) and clean white. No on-screen gibberish text, no fake logos, no
distorted hands or faces.
```

**Colours** — Acacia green `#159947` (primary), Terracotta blue `#2b5ea7`
(secondary), Walnut `#0b1220` (text), Sand `#f8fafc` (background).

**End card for every video:** OshiCart logo on white, the line *"Your Namibian
business, online in minutes"*, and **oshicart.com**. Hold 2 seconds.

**Voice:** warm, direct, Namibian English. Short sentences. Never corporate.
Say "shop" not "SME", "your money" not "revenue".

## Posting order

You don't need all 15 at once. This sequence builds an audience before it
asks for anything:

**Week 1 — get attention**
`01 zero-commission` → `03 orders-on-whatsapp` → `15 free-to-start`

**Week 2 — show it works**
`02 signup` → `04 add-first-product` → `05 share-link-qr`

**Week 3 — go after segments**
`07 salon-bookings` → `08 digital-services` → `06 local-payments`

**Week 4 — depth and recruitment**
`12 statements` → `11 delivery-couriers` → `14 become-an-agent`

**Evergreen / retarget:** `09 browse-stores`, `10 install-as-app`,
`13 refunds`

Post 3–4 a week, 19:00 Namibian time. `01` and `14` are your two strongest —
put any paid budget behind those.

---

## Practical notes

- **Hook is on screen, not spoken.** Most feeds play muted. Every brief puts
  the hook as frame-one text.
- **Subtitles always** — burn them in, don't rely on the platform.
- **Same video, different caption** per platform: Facebook gets the full
  paragraph, TikTok gets one lowercase line. Both are in every brief.
- **TikTok:** post natively, never a Facebook re-upload with a watermark.
- **Facebook:** post as a Reel, not a feed video.
- **Watch retention, not likes.** If viewers drop before 3 seconds the hook is
  wrong — swap it and repost. Hooks are cheap to change; footage isn't.
