Corporate Identity Manual

How the OshiCart brand looks, speaks, and behaves.

Version 1.0 · July 2026 · Octovia Nexus Investments CC

01Brand story & positioning

OshiCart is the Namibian way to sell online. It gives any business — a boutique, a salon, a kapana stand, a side hustle — its own online store link. Customers browse products and order straight to the seller's WhatsApp; the seller gets paid directly through the payment methods Namibians already use. OshiCart takes zero commission.

One-liner (use everywhere)

“Your WhatsApp shop, professional in 2 minutes. Zero commission — customers pay YOU.”

Website headline

“Your Namibian business, online in minutes.”

Brand values

  • Local. Built for Namibia: N$ prices, local payments, local delivery, local language of business.
  • Simple. If a first-time seller can't do it from a phone in 2 minutes, it's too complicated.
  • Honest. No hidden fees, no fake badges, no invented testimonials. Payment-safety warnings are part of the product.
  • Hustle-proud. We celebrate the seller — the market table and the mall shop get the same respect.

02The logo

The OshiCart mark is the Sunrise Cart: the Namibian sun rising inside a shopping cart. It means a new day for your business — sunshine (Namibia, optimism) carried by commerce (the cart), rolling forward on growth-green wheels.

Primary — horizontal lockup (light backgrounds)
Reverse — for walnut/dark backgrounds
Stacked — square placements
Mark only — avatars, favicons, app icon

The wordmark is always set in extra-bold (800): “Oshi” in Walnut Ink, “Cart” in Acacia Green. Never retype it in another font or colour split.

03Logo rules

Clear space

Keep a clear zone around the logo equal to the height of the sun dome (≈ the cart basket). Nothing enters this zone — no text, edges, or other logos.

Minimum sizes

  • Horizontal lockup: 120 px wide on screen / 30 mm in print.
  • Mark only: 16 px (favicon) / 8 mm print.

Approved variants only

BackgroundUse
White / Sand / light photo areaPrimary full-colour lockup
Walnut / darkReverse lockup (white + green)
Acacia green (app icon, avatar)All-white mono mark
Single-colour print / stampsMono black mark

Never

  • ✗ Stretch, squash, rotate, or add drop shadows / glows.
  • ✗ Apply gradients — the old glossy gradient badge is retired.
  • ✗ Recolour the mark or swap the wordmark colours.
  • ✗ Place on busy photos without a white or dark scrim behind it.
  • ✗ Pair with unearned badges (“official partner”, press logos) — truthful marks only.

Voice & tone

  • Plain Namibian English. Write like you talk to a seller at their stall. No e-commerce jargon.
  • Seller-first. “You get paid directly” — benefits before features.
  • Confident, never hypey. Real numbers over superlatives.
  • Local flavour welcome (kapana, hustle, braai) — used naturally, never forced.

06Imagery & applications

Photography & imagery rules

  • Product UI is always real. Screenshots from the live product only — never redrawn or invented interfaces.
  • People & places feel authentically Namibian: real settings (markets, salons, braai stands), golden natural light, natural skin tones. AI-generated lifestyle scenes are permitted for ads if the on-screen UI is a real screenshot.
  • Privacy: customer names/numbers are always pixelated or replaced with fictional ones before publishing.
