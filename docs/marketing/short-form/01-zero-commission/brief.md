## Hook (first 2 seconds — this decides everything)

> **You sold N$10,000 this month. How much did the app take?**

Most people watch with the sound off. The hook must be **on screen as text**,
big, in the first frame — not spoken.

## On-screen text timeline

```
  0:00  "You sold N$10 000 this month."
  0:02  "How much did the app take?"
  0:05  BIG TEXT: N$0
  0:09  "OshiCart takes no commission. Ever."
  0:14  "oshicart.com"
```

## Voiceover script

```
VO (warm, confident, Namibian English):
"You sold ten thousand this month.
 How much did the app take?
 (beat)
 Nothing. Zero.
 OshiCart charges one small monthly fee — and takes no cut of your sales.
 Your money is yours."
```
