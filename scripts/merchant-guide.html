OshiCart
Merchant Guide
Everything in the app, step by step — from opening your store to pulling a statement for your accountant.

1Welcome

OshiCart is a Namibian platform for selling online. You get a store link customers open on their phone, orders arrive on WhatsApp, and customers pay you directly — there is zero OshiCart commission on your sales.

You do not need a website, a domain, or a card machine. Your store link is your website.

2Create your store

The setup wizard takes about two minutes.

  1. Sign up and open the setup wizard.
  2. Enter your store name — what customers see.
  3. Enter your WhatsApp number — where orders and alerts arrive.
  4. Pick your region and town. Customers filter by town, so this decides who finds you.
  5. Choose your industry, which sets your layout and category icons.
  6. Save. Your store link is created immediately: oshicart.com/s/your-store

3Add your first product

  1. Go to Products → Add product.
  2. Give it a clear name and a price in Namibian dollars.
  3. Add a photo — daylight, plain background, product filling the frame. Photos are compressed automatically, so shoot straight from your phone.
  4. Add variants for sizes or colours so customers choose at checkout.
  5. Turn on stock tracking to stop selling when you run out.
  6. Save, then open your store link and check it looks right.

Your store will not appear in Browse Stores until it has at least one product. An empty shop is hidden on purpose — sending customers somewhere with nothing to buy loses them for good.

4Payment methods

  1. Go to Settings → Payments.
  2. Tick what you accept: bank transfer (EFT), cash on delivery or collection, MTC Maris, eWallet, FNB Pay2Cell, or PayToday.
  3. Enter the account or number for every method you tick. For EFT that means the bank and the account number.
  4. Save.

A method with no details cannot be paid, so it is hidden at checkout and cannot be saved. If none of your methods are complete, your checkout closes and buyers are asked to message you — better than an order nobody can pay.

5Delivery and collection

  1. Go to Settings → Delivery.
  2. Turn on collection and add your pickup address.
  3. Turn on your own delivery and set a flat fee.
  4. Turn on Yango or inDrive to let customers order a courier. The buyer pays the driver directly.
  5. Set delivery days and times if you only deliver on certain days.

Yango and inDrive appear only for stores in Windhoek, Swakopmund and Walvis Bay, because that is where they operate.

6Share your store

Your store link is your shop's address on the internet. Your QR code is a picture of that link.

  1. Open Share store in the menu.
  2. Copy link and paste it into your WhatsApp Status, bio, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok.
  3. Share on WhatsApp sends it straight to a customer or group.
  4. Download the QR code and print it on flyers, packaging, your door or your market table.

7Use OshiCart as an app

  1. Android: open your dashboard in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app.
  2. iPhone: open your dashboard in Safari (not Chrome), tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. It installs as OshiCart Dashboard and opens on your orders.

Customers can do the same from any store link. Full instructions: oshicart.com/app

8Handling orders

  1. New orders arrive on WhatsApp and appear under Orders.
  2. Awaiting payment — ordered but not paid yet.
  3. Tap Confirm once you have the money or are happy to go ahead.
  4. Tap Completed when the customer has it. That message carries their invoice.
  5. Record payment when money arrives, with the amount and the date from your bank. Part-payments are fine — record each one.
  6. Cancel if it falls through. Cancelled orders do not count against your monthly limit.

Two taps, and the customer is told at each step — three messages in all: ordered, confirmed, done. Only Cancel asks you to confirm, because it puts stock back and tells the customer the order is off.

Need a Ready step too? Takeaways and shops where "your order is ready for collection" is a real moment can turn the full flow back on under Settings — untick "Use the simple order flow".

Not paid? OshiCart sends two reminders, at 6 hours and the next morning, then cancels after about two days and puts the stock back. The moment you record a payment — or the customer uploads proof — the reminders stop.

9Appointments and bookings

For salons, barbers, and anyone who works by appointment. Customers book a time from a calendar, and each time slot takes one booking — two customers can never book the same slot.

  1. Set your hours under Settings → Availability: tick your working days, then use Generate — opening time, closing time, and session length. 09:00 to 17:00 every hour gives you eight sessions.
  2. When you add a service, choose where it happens — at your place, at the client's place, or online.
  3. Customers book from a real calendar: closed days greyed out, full days struck through, taken times unavailable.
  4. Open Bookings in your dashboard to see who is coming — each day shows its count; tap a day for names and times.
  5. Block a whole day off, or hold back single times for lunch or walk-ins. A booked time cannot be blocked — cancel that order first.
Scheduled deliveries appear on the same calendar, so appointments and drop-offs live in one place.

10Selling online or digital services

Design, marketing, consulting, lessons — work with nothing to deliver and nobody to visit.

  1. Add it like any other item, and where it asks where does this happen, choose Online.
  2. Checkout changes for that customer: no delivery, no collection, no courier, no address. Just their name and WhatsApp number.
  3. Work by appointment (a coaching call, a lesson)? Set your times under Settings → Availability and the customer picks a slot.
  4. Work on projects instead (a logo, a website, a campaign)? Leave availability off — checkout tells the customer you will contact them on WhatsApp to get started.
A store can mix them freely: in-chair haircuts, call-outs to a client's home, and online consultations side by side. Each item carries its own setting.

11Hiring things out (rentals)

Tents, tools, sound gear, dresses — anything customers borrow and bring back.

  1. Add the item and choose For hire. Say how many you own, and the shortest and longest hire you allow.
  2. Choose how it is charged: per day (tools, tents, dresses — first and last day both count: 3 days at N$150 is N$450) or per night (rooms — check-in to check-out, and the check-out day is free, so a new guest can arrive that same day).
  3. OshiCart counts what is already out. If both your tents are hired for those dates, a third customer is told "only 0 available for those dates" and cannot book — even two people ordering in the same second cannot both take the last one.
  4. Need time to clean or check the item between hires? Set days between hires and OshiCart keeps that gap free automatically. At 0, back-to-back hires are allowed, and a cancelled hire frees its dates immediately.
  5. Ask for a refundable deposit if you want security on the item. It is added to what the customer pays — its own line at checkout and on the invoice, never taxed — and you hand it back when the item returns in good shape.
  6. Need the customer to bring something — a driver's licence, proof of address? Type it in Documents the customer must bring and it appears on the product, at checkout, and in the WhatsApp order.
  7. For higher-value hires you can also tick Ask for the hirer's ID number. The customer types it at checkout, you see it on the order, and it appears nowhere else — not on their invoice, not on their tracking page. When you record the return, OshiCart offers to delete it, because once the item is back the number is a risk to hold rather than a record worth keeping. Only ask for it when you genuinely need it.
  8. On Orders, each hired line shows its dates — Hire · 3 days · 20–22 Aug — so you always know what is out and when it is due back.
  9. When it comes back, tap Record return on the order: note which unit went out (registration, asset tag, room name), the date, and its condition. If it is late, OshiCart counts the days and suggests the late fee you set — hold it back by refunding a smaller deposit, and the credit note shows it automatically.
Hiring never touches your stock number. "How many you own" is how many can be out at the same time, not how many are left to sell.

12Invoices

Every order has a proper invoice. Open the order and tap View invoice, then Print or save as PDF.

Enter a VAT number in Settings and invoices become proper tax invoices showing VAT at 15% and your VAT number.

13Refunds and credit notes

When you give money back, record it so your books and your bank still agree.

  1. Open Orders and find the order. Record refund sits next to Record payment, and only appears once the customer has paid something — you cannot refund money you never received.
  2. Enter the amount, the date the money actually left your account, and how you sent it back. Part of an order or all of it.
  3. A credit note is created automatically and appears on the order. Open it to print or save as PDF for the customer.
  4. Your statement updates itself: the refund shows under Refunded, and the order counts as owing again so Outstanding stays honest.
A credit note is the document that cancels part of an invoice. If you are registered for VAT you may not simply delete an invoice you have issued — you issue a credit note against it, and the VAT is reversed on it too. OshiCart works that out for you.

14Your customers

Everyone who orders is saved automatically. Open Customers to see how often each person orders and what they have spent, and to message them on WhatsApp.

Your customer list is yours alone. It is never shared with other merchants.

15WhatsApp broadcasts

  1. Open Broadcast.
  2. Choose everyone, repeat customers, or recent customers.
  3. Pick a sample message or write your own. Samples already include your store link.
  4. Send. Messages go from your own WhatsApp number, so customers recognise you.

16Reviews

Reviews appear on your storefront under your products. Open Reviews to read them.

Only verified buyers can review, so a rating on OshiCart means someone really shopped with you.

17Coupons and discounts

  1. Open Coupons.
  2. Pick a starter template — welcome offer, spend-and-save, launch promo, or delivery-on-us — and the form fills in.
  3. Change the code, amount, minimum order, usage limit or expiry.
  4. Save. Nothing goes live until you press save.
  5. Share the code in a broadcast, on your Status, or on a flyer.

18Adding many products at once

  1. Go to Products → Import.
  2. Download the template spreadsheet so your columns are right.
  3. Fill it in, save as CSV, and upload.
  4. Check the preview and confirm. Anything with a problem is listed so you can fix it.

19Abandoned checkout recovery Oshi-Automate and Oshi-Pro

When a customer fills in checkout but does not finish, OshiCart sends them a WhatsApp reminder. Turn it on in Settings; reminders then go out automatically.

20Statements for your books Oshi-Automate and Oshi-Pro

  1. Open Statements and choose a month. On Oshi-Pro you can also pick Last 12 months for a whole year in one document.
  2. You get totals for sales, VAT and delivery, a breakdown by status and payment method, and every order listed.
  3. Check Received against your bank: what came in that month, what is still outstanding, and how many orders remain unpaid.
  4. Print or save as PDF for a paper copy, or Download spreadsheet for your bookkeeper.

Received counts payments dated in that month, whichever month the order was placed — that is the figure to compare with your bank. It is only as complete as your record-keeping, so mark payments as they come in.

21Analytics

Open Analytics to see store visits, orders, revenue, and which products people look at most.

22Your plan and limits

  1. Open Subscription to see your plan and what you have used.
  2. Your order allowance resets every month on your billing date — the page tells you when.
  3. Cancelled orders do not count. Only real orders use your allowance.
  4. Upgrade any time for more products, more orders, or the higher-tier features.

23Selling rules

To keep OshiCart safe and trusted, some items are prohibited: adult content, illegal drugs, counterfeit goods, fraud, and dangerous or restricted items. Stores that break the rules can be hidden or suspended.

Read the full list before you list: oshicart.com/prohibited-products
