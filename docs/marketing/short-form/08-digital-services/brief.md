# 08. Designers & consultants — sell work with nothing to deliver

🎬 **Flow + screen recording** — Flow makes the opener and closer; you record the app in the middle. Flow cannot render the real dashboard.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Designers, marketers, consultants, tutors, VAs |
| **Why this one** | A segment nobody pitches. Big opportunity, low competition. |
| **Length** | 22s |
| **Format** | 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 |

---

## Hook (first 2 seconds — this decides everything)

> **You don't sell products. You still need this.**

Most people watch with the sound off. The hook must be **on screen as text**,
big, in the first frame — not spoken.

## On-screen text timeline

```
  0:00  "Graphic designer? Consultant? Tutor?"
  0:03  "Nothing to deliver. Nobody to visit."
  0:07  "Mark it ONLINE — checkout knows."
  0:12  "No delivery. No address. No courier."
  0:18  "Just your work, and a client."
```
