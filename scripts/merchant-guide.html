OshiCart
Merchant Setup Guide
Set up your store, get paid, and start selling — in minutes.

1Welcome

OshiCart is a proudly Namibian platform that lets you sell online through a clean store link and simple WhatsApp orders. There is zero OshiCart commission on your sales — customers pay you directly, so every dollar you earn stays with you. This guide walks you through everything you need to launch and start selling.

2Create your store (under 2 minutes)

Head to the setup wizard and enter just three things to go live:

  • Store name — what customers will see at the top of your store.
  • Your WhatsApp number — this is where customers contact you about orders.
  • Your industry — so your store uses the right wording for what you sell.

That's it. Your store goes live at a shareable link: oshicart.com/s/your-store.

3Add products & great photos

Add up to 3 photos per product. Good, bright, square photos sell best, so shoot in daylight against a clean background. The app automatically compresses your photos, so you can upload straight from your phone — no editing needed.

  • Set a clear price for every item.
  • Add variations (for example size or colour) when a product comes in options.
  • You can also list Services, not just physical products.

4Payment methods you can accept

Enable any of the payment options below. For each one, you simply enter the number customers should send money to.

Bank Transfer (EFT) MTC Maris FNB Pay2Cell eWallet PayToday Cash on Delivery

eWallet covers FNB eWallet, BlueWallet, EasyWallet, Nedbank Money, and PayPulse.

Important: Always confirm the money is actually in your bank account or wallet before you hand over any goods. Proof-of-payment screenshots can be faked — only trust your real balance.

5Delivery options

Choose what buyers see at checkout:

  • Store delivery — you deliver the order yourself and set your own delivery fee.
  • Yango / inDrive — the buyer books and pays the courier directly. You just prepare the parcel for collection.

If you offer Yango or inDrive, set your pickup address so couriers know exactly where to collect.

6Share your store

Your store link works everywhere, and customers never need to install an app. Spread it as widely as you can:

  • Post your link on WhatsApp Status.
  • Share your built-in QR code in person or on flyers.
  • Post it on Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.

The same single link works on every platform — no app needed for your customers.

7Selling rules

To keep OshiCart safe and trusted, some items are prohibited: adult content, illegal drugs, counterfeit goods, fraud, and dangerous or restricted items. Stores that break the rules can be hidden or suspended.

Read the full list before you list: oshicart.com/prohibited-products.
